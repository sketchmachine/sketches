I've been thinking for a while now, why do pictures need to be *sharp*? Apart from showing and capturing details well, why is our instinct to worship clarity?

These pictures are taken on my Canon 800D (an entry level camera) that I've been using since 2018. Recently, I've been fascinated with pancake lenses, particularly the type that recycles the plastic lens from a disposable film camera.

The image lacks clarity. The plastic lens is clearly inferior, and some might say, a waste of the sensor on my DSLR. It's got its perks though. It's so flat. Imperceptible. 

We don't need clarity to see colour.
